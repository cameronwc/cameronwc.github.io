Questions, answered.

Why was Suzfit made?

It was built for the developer's fiancée, who was tired of bloated gym apps and wanted a simple way to log her workouts. It grew from there and is shared free with anyone who wants the same.

Is Suzfit free?

Yes. Everything is free: unlimited saved workouts, the full exercise library, weight suggestions, analytics and iCloud sync. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. Suzfit works the moment you open it. Your data lives on your iPhone and, if iCloud is enabled, in your own private iCloud account.

How do weight suggestions work?

Suzfit shows what you lifted for each set last time and applies your progression rule to suggest the next weight. Double progression adds weight once every set hits the top of the rep range; linear adds weight every session you hit the minimum; RPE adjusts to how hard the last session felt. Set it per workout or override per exercise.

Can I add HIIT to a strength workout?

Yes. Add finishers like mountain climbers or box jumps to the end of any strength plan with their own sets and rep ranges.

Does the rest timer work when my phone is locked?

Yes. It schedules a local notification, so you get an alert when rest ends even if the screen is off or you are in another app. That is the only reason Suzfit asks for notification permission.

Where is my data stored?

On your device and in your own private iCloud database. Cooper Industries has no servers and cannot see your workouts. Details in the privacy policy.

Does Suzfit support kilograms?

Version 1.0 logs in pounds. A full kilogram mode is planned.
