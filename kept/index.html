Kept

Privacy Policy

Kept collects nothing. There is no Kept server, no account, no analytics, and no third-party code. Your prayers never leave your phone except through your own iCloud. The App Store privacy label for Kept is "Data Not Collected."

What Kept collects

Nothing. Kept has no server, no account system, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising, and no third-party code. The developer cannot see, receive, or recover anything you write.

Where your prayers live

  • On your device. Everything you write is stored in Kept's own container on your iPhone, protected by the device's encryption and, if you leave the default on, by Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.
  • In your own iCloud, if you choose. If iCloud is turned on for Kept, your journal syncs between your own devices through CloudKit's private database. That data belongs to your Apple ID. Apple encrypts it in transit and at rest, and the developer has no access to it. Turning iCloud off for Kept in iOS Settings stops sync.
  • In local backups. Kept keeps a few JSON copies of your journal inside its own app container so a failed update can never take your journal away. These never leave the device except through your own iCloud device backup.

Scripture matching

Kept suggests Bible verses that speak to what you wrote. This runs entirely on the device using a small language model and the full text of two public-domain translations (World English Bible and King James Version). It works in airplane mode. No text is sent anywhere to do this.

The one optional feature that can send text

"Deeper reflection" can send the text of one prayer at a time to an external reflection service to receive a longer written reflection. It is:

  • off by default, and stays off unless you turn it on in Settings;
  • confirmed per prayer: every time, Kept shows you the exact text that would be sent before anything leaves the device;
  • minimal: only the prayer text and any attached verse references are sent. No name, no dates, no subjects, no other prayers, no device identifiers.

In the current release the reflection service is not connected, so this feature cannot transmit anything. If a service is connected in a future release, this policy will name it and describe its retention terms before the feature is enabled.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Kept schedules a local notification on your device. Its text is fixed and never includes anything you wrote. During a Kept+ free trial, Kept also schedules local reminders before the trial converts to a paid subscription.

Purchases

Kept+ is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Kept checks your subscription status with Apple's StoreKit on the device. The developer receives only the anonymized sales reporting Apple provides to all developers and never sees your payment details.

Your control

  • Export your whole journal as Markdown or PDF at any time from Settings.
  • Delete any prayer. It sits in the Trash for 30 days, then is removed for good.
  • Delete everything by deleting the app; if iCloud sync was on, also delete Kept's data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

Kept is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
