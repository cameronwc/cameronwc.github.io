Plan it. Track it. Shop it.

Most meal planners stop at the recipe. Preplate carries your targets all the way through to the grocery aisle.

Plan your week

Assign recipes to lunch and dinner slots. Meal Prep mode fills Monday to Friday in one tap and scales every serving to match. Save the week as a template and reuse it.

Track your macros

Log by search, barcode, a photo of your plate, a snap of a restaurant menu, or just describe what you ate. Calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber and sodium against your daily targets.

Shop smarter

Your plan becomes a grocery list automatically: quantities scaled, duplicates merged, recurring staples added, everything sorted by aisle. At Kroger-family stores it follows the real aisle order of your store.

AI that actually helps

Describe your week and the AI planner builds dinners around your targets. The coach answers questions about your log. The recipe creator turns "high-protein, 20 minutes, no dairy" into something you can cook tonight.
