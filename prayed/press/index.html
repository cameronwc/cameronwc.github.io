Prayed: a private prayer journal that remembers.

Everything a writer needs to cover Prayed: the facts, the screenshots, the icon, and a real person to email. Nothing here needs permission to use.

In one paragraph

Prayed is a prayer journal for iPhone and Apple Watch from Cooper Industries, an independent developer. You write what you are praying for, and Prayed matches three Bible verses to it on the phone itself, then brings the prayer back to you at three days, a week, a month and a year so you can see what you asked for beside what came of it. It has no server and no account, so nothing a person writes can leave their device except through their own iCloud. Writing is free with no cap; Prayed Plus, which remembers a whole history rather than the last thirty days, is $34.99 a year or $5.99 a month with a seven-day trial. Version 2.0, released September 11, 2026, added Apple Watch, Siri, bring-back-on-a-date reminders, and month and year reviews.

Why it is different

Most prayer apps are either a Bible reader with a notes field or a social feed of other people's requests. Prayed is neither. It is built around one idea: a prayer you wrote should come back to you later, dated, so that the answer is visible. And it takes the privacy of that writing seriously in a way the category mostly does not. There is no Prayed server to breach, no account to leak, and the scripture matching runs on a small model inside the app, so it works in airplane mode and never sends text anywhere. The only third-party code is anonymous usage counts, and the App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.

From the developer

"I built Prayed because I kept praying for the same things and forgetting that I had. The app's whole job is to hand a prayer back to you a year later, so you can see what happened."

"A prayer journal is the most private thing a person can write. I did not want to be able to read it, so I built it so that I can't. There is no server, and there never will be."
