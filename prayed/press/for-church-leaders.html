Prayed: a prayer journal your people can trust

A free iPhone app that helps people keep praying for the same things, and see what came of it. There is no server and no account. What someone writes stays on their phone.

What it does

  • Write a prayer in a sentence or two. Tag who it is for.
  • It comes back at three days, a week, a month and a year, so the person sees what they asked for beside what happened.
  • Three verses are matched to each prayer, on the phone, from the World English Bible or the King James Version.
  • Mark it open, ongoing, answered or released. Answered prayers build into a timeline for the hard days.
  • Every prayer for one person gathers on one page, from the first ask to the answer.
  • Apple Watch, Siri and widgets for a quick daily check-in. No streak shaming, ever.

What it costs

Writing is free with no limit on prayers, forever. The free plan brings back the last thirty days. Prayed Plus remembers a whole history and adds reviews and export for $34.99 a year or $5.99 a month, with a week free to try. There are no ads and no in-app purchases beyond that one subscription.

Why it is safe to recommend

  • No server. There is nowhere for prayers to be stored except the person's own phone and, if they choose, their own iCloud.
  • No account. No email, no password, no sign-in. Nothing to leak.
  • Nothing is read or sent. The verse matching runs inside the app and works in airplane mode. The developer cannot see, receive or recover anything a person writes.
  • Face ID lock is on by default.
  • App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. The only third-party code is anonymous usage counts, never text.
  • No social features. There is no feed and no sharing of prayer text. A month or year review can share a card with numbers and first names only.

For a small group or a prayer team

Prayed is personal by design; it does not have shared lists. What works well is each member keeping their own journal and using the weekly review of open prayers as the prompt for what to bring to the group.
