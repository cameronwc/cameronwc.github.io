Getting Started

Prayed is a private prayer journal that remembers. Write what you're praying for, and Prayed brings it back to you at three days, a week, two weeks, a month, three months, six months, a year, and two years, so you see what you asked for and what came of it. Mark each prayer Open, Ongoing, Answered, or Released; answered prayers build into a timeline on the Answered tab.

Common Questions

Nothing shows up on Today yet.

Prayed starts bringing prayers back three days after they were written. Backdate a prayer (the date field on the Write screen, or the "Since" date during setup) and it enters the ladder from that date.

How does scripture matching work? Is my prayer sent anywhere?

No. Matching runs on your phone using a small language model and the full text of two public-domain Bibles. The first time you use it, Prayed downloads about 35 MB of matching data from Apple's servers (not from us); after that it works offline, even in airplane mode. The free plan includes three matches; Prayed Plus matches every prayer.

Where are my prayers stored?

On your device, and in your own iCloud if iCloud is on for Prayed. There is no Prayed server and no account. See the privacy policy.

My prayers aren't syncing between devices.

Both devices need the same Apple ID, iCloud Drive turned on, and Prayed enabled under iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud. Sync can take a few minutes after you open the app on the second device.

I deleted a prayer by mistake.

Settings → Your data → Trash. Prayers stay there for 30 days and can be put back with one tap.

Can I get my prayers out of Prayed?

Yes, any time. Settings → Your data → Export as Markdown or Export as PDF. Prayed also keeps local backups (Settings → Your data → Backups) that can be restored.

How do I import an old journal?

During setup, or from Settings → Your data → Import from a file. Prayed reads plain text (one entry per blank-line-separated block, with a date on the first line if you have one) and Day One JSON exports. Dates come across so old entries start resurfacing right away.

How do I restore my Prayed Plus purchase?

Settings → Prayed Plus → Restore purchases. Be sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you originally purchased with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us. Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Prayed. Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the paid period. Everything you wrote stays on your device either way.

The widgets say they need Prayed Plus.

Widgets are part of Prayed Plus. Tap the widget to start a free week; they update the next time you open the app.
