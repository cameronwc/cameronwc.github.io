Bible verses of gratitude, and how to keep a record that lasts

Thirteen verses on giving thanks, arranged for the ordinary morning, the answered prayer, and the hard season when thanks is an act of will. With a prayer to write for each.

September 11, 20266 min read13 verses, World English Bible

Gratitude is the easiest prayer to recommend and the hardest to sustain. Everyone agrees you should give thanks. Almost no one does it past the third day of a gratitude journal. The problem is not a lack of things to be thankful for. It is that thankfulness, unlike worry, does not remind you of itself.

The verses below are quoted word for word from the World English Bible and arranged for the three situations where thanks actually gets prayed: the ordinary morning when nothing is wrong, the day a prayer is answered, and the hard season when giving thanks is a decision rather than a feeling. The last group is the one that matters most and the one gratitude journals never mention.

Each group ends with a prayer you could write. The ordinary ones are worth keeping most, because they are the ones you will not remember.

The ordinary morning

These are the verses for a day with nothing in it. Rain, coffee, the garden, the fact that everyone is home. Scripture treats the ordinary as a gift with a giver, and that is the whole shift.

This is the day that the LORD has made. We will rejoice and be glad in it!

Psalm 118:24 · WEB

This is the day. Not a special one. Whichever one you are reading this on.

Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of lights, with whom can be no variation nor turning shadow.

James 1:17 · WEB

Every good gift. The small ones count, and they all have the same source.

You visit the earth, and water it. You greatly enrich it. The river of God is full of water. You provide them grain, for so you have ordained it.

Psalm 65:9 · WEB

Rain, rivers, grain. A verse for anyone with a garden, or a window.

It is a good thing to give thanks to the LORD, to sing praises to your name, Most High,

Psalm 92:1 · WEB

A good thing. Giving thanks is not only a duty. It is good for the one doing it.

A prayer to write

Thank you for a quiet morning. For the rain, for the coffee, for the fact that nothing is wrong today. I am writing this down because I will not remember it, and I want to.

When a prayer is answered

The moment of an answered prayer is the moment gratitude comes easily, and also the moment it evaporates fastest. Within a week the answer feels like it was always going to happen. These verses are about telling what God has done, out loud and on paper, before the memory softens.

I will give thanks to the LORD with my whole heart. I will tell of all your marvelous works.

Psalm 9:1 · WEB

Tell of all your marvelous works. Telling is the verb. An answered prayer is meant to be recounted.

Praise the LORD, my soul, and don’t forget all his benefits,

Psalm 103:2 · WEB

Don't forget all his benefits. The psalmist knows you will. That is why the verse exists.

Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, for his loving kindness endures forever.

Psalm 107:1 · WEB

The refrain of a psalm that lists, one after another, the people God rescued and what they were rescued from. A model for your own list.

Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise. Give thanks to him, and bless his name.

Psalm 100:4 · WEB

Thanksgiving at the gates, praise in the courts. Thanks is the way in.

A prayer to write

You answered. The scan was clear. I am writing it down today, while I still feel it, so that six months from now I cannot pretend it was luck. Thank you. I will tell someone.

The hard season

In everything give thanks, Paul writes, and he wrote it from a life that included prison and shipwreck. This is not gratitude as a mood. It is gratitude as a decision made in the dark, and these verses are the ones people hold onto when the feeling has gone.

In everything give thanks, for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus toward you.

1 Thessalonians 5:18 · WEB

In everything. Not for everything. The thanks is given inside the hard thing, not about it.

It is because of the LORD’s loving kindnesses that we are not consumed, because his mercies don’t fail.

Lamentations 3:22 · WEB

From the book of Lamentations. Not consumed is the whole thanksgiving. Some seasons, that is enough.

And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to which also you were called in one body, and be thankful.

Colossians 3:15 · WEB

Peace rules, and be thankful is attached to it. The two are given together.

In nothing be anxious, but in everything, by prayer and petition with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God.

Philippians 4:6 · WEB

With thanksgiving, in the same breath as the request. You can ask for the thing you do not have and give thanks in the same sentence. Paul did.

giving thanks always concerning all things in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to God, even the Father;

Ephesians 5:20 · WEB

Always, concerning all things. The most demanding verse on this page, and the one that means the most in the year you can finally pray it.

A prayer to write

I do not feel thankful and I am going to give thanks anyway. We are not consumed. That is what I have today. Thank you for that. Thank you for being here in the middle of this, since you have not taken it away.

Keep a record of what he has done

The Psalms keep telling the reader to remember, and they keep telling it because remembering does not happen on its own. The ordinary mornings are forgotten. The answered prayers turn into things that were always going to work out. The hard seasons are survived and then the surviving is forgotten too.
