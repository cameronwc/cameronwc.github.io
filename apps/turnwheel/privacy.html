Turnwheel Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-07-29

Turnwheel does not require an account and does not collect personal information for its own use. Your content stays on your device.

Advertising

Turnwheel shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising provider. AdMob may collect and process device information — including advertising identifiers (IDFA) — to serve and measure ads. This data is handled under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Tracking and Your Choices

On iOS, Turnwheel asks permission through App Tracking Transparency before any tracking occurs. You can decline the prompt, or opt out at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; ads will still be shown, but they will not be personalized using your advertising identifier.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
