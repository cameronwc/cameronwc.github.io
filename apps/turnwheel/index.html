Turnwheel

Group decisions, on repeat

Save chore rotations, lunch spots, and standup order as wheels. Spin with real haptics, check spin history, and let Fairness Mode balance turns over time.

Turnwheel turns "whose turn is it?" into a five-second spin.

Build a wheel for anything your group decides on repeatedly — chore rotations, lunch spots, who runs standup, who takes out the trash. Name it, add your options, and spin. The wheel stays saved, so next time it's a tap, not a re-negotiation.

HOW IT WORKS Create a named wheel and list its options. Spin the wheel with a tap or a swipe — the wheel spins with real animation and haptic feedback, then lands on a winner shown in a large result banner. Spin again, edit the wheel, or share the result as an image. Every wheel keeps its own spin history, so you can see what's been picked recently.

FAIRNESS MODE Standard spins are genuinely random, every time — no patterns, no way to game it. Fairness Mode changes the odds instead: options that have come up less recently get better odds, so chores and turns even out over weeks instead of clustering on the same person. Toggle it per wheel.

BUILT FOR REPEAT USE Turnwheel isn't a novelty spinner you open once. Wheels are meant to be reused — for the Tuesday lunch order, the weekly chore list, the standup rotation — so your setup work pays off every time you open the app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN Everything lives on your device. No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics. Your wheels and their history never leave your phone.

WHAT'S INCLUDED - Unlimited wheels with named, reorderable option lists - Animated spin with haptic feedback and a clear result banner - Per-wheel spin history - Fairness Mode for weighted, self-balancing rotation - Share results as an image from the native share sheet - One free saved wheel; unlock more slots, premium wheel skins and spin animations, and Fairness Mode by watching a short video, or keep using standard mode for free

Turnwheel includes ads to keep the app free. A banner shows on the wheel list, and an occasional interstitial may appear between spins — never during the spin animation itself.
