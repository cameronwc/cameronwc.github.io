How to submit.

No portal, no form. An email and a link.

  1. Shoot, or pull from your archive.10 to 15 frames, one pose each, from sessions where every identifiable person has signed the model release.
  2. Strip EXIF and fill the sheet.One row per frame: category, subject count, light, location, release on file. Copy the CSV template from the spec.
  3. Email us.Send to support@cooperindustries.cc with the subject Prompted contributor, a link to the frames (Dropbox, Drive, iCloud, WeTransfer), the sheet, and the credit name you want shown.
  4. We curate and write the prompts.We pick the frames that fill gaps in the library and read clearly at phone size, then write the setup steps and three verbal prompts in the app's voice. Your notes on what you said to get the pose help a lot.
  5. You approve your credit, then it ships.You get a preview of every accepted pose with "Photo: your name" before publish. Accepted frames go out in the next catalog update and lifetime Pro lands on your Apple Account.

Questions, answered.

Do I keep copyright?

Yes. You keep the copyright and every other right in your photographs. You grant Cooper Industries a non-exclusive license to use accepted frames in the Prompted app and its marketing, and you can keep selling and licensing the same frames to anyone else.

Can I contribute if the models are minors?

Yes, with a model release signed by a parent or legal guardian for each minor. The release we provide has a guardian section. We never publish a minor's name, and senior sessions are one of the categories we most want.

What if a frame is declined?

Declined frames are deleted from our systems and are not covered by the license. A set of 10 to 15 accepted frames earns lifetime Pro; if fewer are accepted on a first pass, send more to reach the threshold.

How is credit shown?

Each pose detail screen in the app shows "Photo: your name" under the reference image. You choose the name, personal or business, and you see the credit line before anything is published. Marketing uses credit you where practical; a Pinterest pin or App Store screenshot may not always have room.

Can I change my mind later?

Uses already published stay published, in the app versions and posts that are already out. You can ask us in writing to stop new publication of a frame and we will pull it from future catalog updates and new marketing within 30 days. Lifetime Pro already granted is yours.

Do I need to write the prompts?

No. Every pose in Prompted gets setup steps and three verbal prompts written by us, in a consistent voice. Tell us what you said to get the pose and we'll work from that.

Got a set ready?
