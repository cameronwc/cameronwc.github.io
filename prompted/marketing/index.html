Built for the moment your clients are waiting.

One-handed, mid-shoot, in whatever light you've got. Prompted is a posing reference, not a CRM.

Poses matched to your light

A local solar engine computes the sun's position from your coordinates, entirely on your phone. The sun chip tells you what light you're in, and one tap filters the library to poses that actually work in it. No weather service, no connection, no account.

Word-for-word prompts

Every pose ships with prompts in three tones, playful, romantic, and calm for nervous clients, plus silent setup steps for you. The prompt is what you say out loud. The setup is what you do. They never mix.

Nervous clientYou don't have to look at the camera. Just look at each other.

Filter like you shoot

Couples, engagement, family, maternity, senior portraits. By subject count, location, orientation, difficulty, and by who's in front of the lens: seated variants, plus-size flattering, wheelchair, limited mobility, and late-term poses.

Plan the shoot, then run it

Build a session, order the shots, and open Shoot Mode: full screen, portrait-locked, screen stays awake, one-tap brightness for bright sun. Swipe through your list with the prompt overlaid on every frame.

Built for the field

A barn with no bars is exactly where you need your shot list.
